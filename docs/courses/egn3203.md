## Units

**Unit 1. Numbers on a computer**

- Floating point; round-off and truncation error; cancellation.
- `float`: (1 − cos x)/x² near x = 0, written two ways. Shows catastrophic cancellation and its fix.
  Check: the series value.

**Unit 2. Roots of equations**

- Bisection, Newton, secant; convergence order; failure modes.
- `newton`: Newton's method on a function with a draggable start. Shows quadratic convergence near a
  simple root and the basins that send a start elsewhere. Check: the error ratio e_{n+1}/e_n² tending
  to |f″/2f′|.
- Application lab, `area-mach`: a supersonic nozzle's area–Mach relation solved for the exit Mach at
  an area ratio of 3. Shows why the starting guess picks the subsonic or supersonic branch. Check:
  `thermo`'s own inversion.

**Unit 3. Linear systems**

- Gaussian elimination; LU; pivoting; condition number; iterative methods.
- `conditioning`: a nearly singular 2×2 system drawn as two lines. Shows that a tiny change in b
  moves the solution far when the lines are nearly parallel. Check: the observed amplification
  against cond(A).
- Application: the truss from Statics solved as a linear system (a link, not a new lab).

**Unit 4. Interpolation and least squares**

- Polynomial interpolation; Runge's phenomenon; splines; linear regression; normal equations.
- `runge`: equally spaced against Chebyshev nodes. Shows the edge oscillation growing with degree on
  equal spacing only. Check: the max error computed on a fine grid.
- `fit`: a least-squares line and polynomial through noisy data. Shows overfitting as degree rises.
  Check: QR against the normal equations. Links STA 3032's regression.
- Application: fitting a pump's head–flow curve from test points, and reading the operating point
  where it meets the pipe's system curve.

**Unit 5. Differentiation and integration**

- Finite differences; trapezoid and Simpson; Gauss quadrature; error order.
- `order`: error against h on log–log axes. Shows the slope equals the method's order, until
  round-off takes over at small h. Check: the fitted slope.

**Unit 6. Ordinary differential equations**

- Euler; Runge–Kutta; stability; stiffness; systems.
- `stability`: explicit and implicit Euler on y′ = λy. Shows explicit Euler blowing up when |1 + hλ| > 1,
  and implicit Euler staying bounded. Check: the amplification factor against the observed growth.
- Application lab, `blowdown-numeric`: a compressed-air tank's blowdown integrated by Euler and RK4
  at several steps, against the closed form from MAP 2302.

**Unit 7. Monte Carlo** (if covered)

- Random sampling; error falling as 1/√N.
- `monte-carlo`: estimating an area by sampling. Shows the error halving only when N quadruples.
  Check: the observed spread against σ/√N.
