## Units

**Unit 1. Machining**

- Orthogonal cutting; Merchant's force circle; power; Taylor tool life; machining time and cost;
  the economic cutting speed.
- `tool-life`: cutting speed against tool life and cost per part. Shows a cost minimum between
  "fast and wearing tools" and "slow and paying for time". Check: the analytic optimum against a
  numerical minimum.
- Application: machining the engine's chamber from bar stock: material removed, time and tool cost.
  Real dimensions from the engine repo.

**Unit 2. Casting and moulding**

- Solidification time (Chvorinov); risers; injection-moulding fill and cooling time; shrinkage.
- `chvorinov`: casting shapes of equal volume. Shows that the volume-to-area ratio, not the volume,
  sets solidification time, so a riser must have a larger ratio than the part. Check: the
  rule against a 1D conduction solution with a stated constant.
- `cooling`: moulding cooling time against wall thickness. Shows the time growing with thickness
  squared. Check: the slab conduction solution.

**Unit 3. Forming**

- Sheet bending; springback; deep drawing limits; rolling and forging forces in brief.
- `springback`: bend angle against radius-to-thickness ratio and material. Shows that a stronger,
  less stiff material springs back more. Check: the elastic-unloading formula against a moment–
  curvature integration.

**Unit 4. Tolerances and variation**

- Tolerance stack-up (worst case and statistical); process capability (C_p, C_pk); statistical
  process control.
- `stack-up`: a chain of parts with tolerances. Shows the root-sum-square stack much tighter than
  the worst case, and when that assumption fails. Check: Monte Carlo of the stack.
- `capability`: a process mean shifting inside its limits. Shows the yield falling with C_pk, not
  C_p. Check: the normal distribution from `stats`. Links STA 3032.
- Application lab, `orifice-tolerance`: an injector orifice drilled to a diameter tolerance. Choked
  mass flow scales with area, so a ±1% diameter tolerance becomes about ±2% flow and shifts O/F.
  Real orifice size and O/F from the engine repo. Check: the stand sim's orifice lab.

**Unit 5. Cost, rate and process selection**

- Fixed and variable cost; break-even volume between processes; learning curves; additive
  manufacturing.
- `break-even`: machining against casting against printing as volume rises. Shows each process
  winning in its own range. Check: the crossover volumes by root finding.
