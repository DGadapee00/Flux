## OCW map and coverage (D23)

18.02SC runs four units: vectors and matrices with parametric curves; partial derivatives with
Lagrange multipliers; double integrals, vector fields, line integrals and Green's theorem; triple
integrals, flux, the divergence theorem and Stokes' theorem. The built course (Merino's order) has
nothing on Lagrange multipliers, vector fields, line integrals, Green, flux, divergence or Stokes,
about 40% of 18.02SC. Under D23 those become new units, and the units are re-ordered to 18.02SC's,
with OpenStax *Calculus Volume 3* cited for them. Merino's notation stays (`CONVENTIONS.md`).
