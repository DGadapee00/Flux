## Sources

Order of authority: the OCW program, then the textbook (D23, D28).

1. **OCW program:** [18.01SC Single Variable Calculus, Fall 2010](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-01sc-single-variable-calculus-fall-2010/):
   lecture videos, course notes, problem sets with solutions, exams with solutions. Its five units
   set this module's units and order.
2. **Textbooks:** OpenStax *Calculus Volume 1* and *Volume 2* (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), cited by section
   (D7). Volume 2 Ch 5–6 supply sequences and the convergence tests, which 18.01SC does not teach
   beyond Taylor series.

## Units

Following 18.01SC. The labs already built are placed where 18.01SC teaches their topic.

**Unit 1. Differentiation** (18.01SC Unit 1; OpenStax Vol 1 Ch 2–3)

- Limits and continuity, inside the definition of the derivative (18.01SC has no separate limits
  unit); rules; chain rule; implicit differentiation; inverse functions, exponentials and logs.
- Labs: `limits`, `secant`, `focus` (50 mm camera lens; nominal focal length and close-focus scale from Nikon specifications).

**Unit 2. Applications of differentiation** (18.01SC Unit 2; Vol 1 Ch 4)

- Linear and quadratic approximation; curve sketching; optimization; related rates; Newton's method;
  the mean value theorem; antiderivatives; first differential equations.
- Labs: `rates`, `box`, `can` (330 mL drink-can capacity, cited from the manufacturer’s linked package listing).

**Unit 3. The definite integral and its applications** (18.01SC Unit 3; Vol 1 Ch 5–6)

- Riemann sums; the definite integral; both fundamental theorems; areas; volumes by disks, washers
  and shells; average value; probability; numerical integration.
- Labs: `riemann`, `accum`, `solids`, `numint`, `barrel` (50 US gallon Rain Wizard capacity), `spheroid` (disk/shell checks against Session 58).

**Unit 4. Techniques of integration** (18.01SC Unit 4A–B; Vol 2 Ch 2–3)

- Trigonometric powers; trigonometric substitution; completing the square; partial fractions;
  integration by parts; arc length; surface area.
- Labs: `arc`.

**Unit 5. Parametric equations and polar coordinates** (18.01SC Unit 4C; Vol 2 Ch 7)

- Parametric curves; polar curves; area and arc length in polar.
- Labs: `parametric`, `polar`.

**Unit 6. Exploring the infinite** (18.01SC Unit 5; Vol 1 §4.8, Vol 2 Ch 3.7 and Ch 5–6)

- L'Hôpital's rule; improper integrals; sequences; series and the convergence tests (from OpenStax);
  power series; Taylor series.
- Labs: `improper`, `taylor`, `series`.

## What moves

- L'Hôpital leaves Calc I's applications unit for Unit 6.
- Improper integrals leave the techniques unit for Unit 6.
- Volumes and numerical integration join the definite-integral unit.
- Parametric and polar come before series.
- The eight notes files are re-cut into six, keeping their worked examples, `fix` and `out` boxes.
