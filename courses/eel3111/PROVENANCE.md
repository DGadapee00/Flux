# Provenance

EEL 3111 Circuit Analysis I lives in this folder. There is no earlier repo to snapshot. The course is
written here, unit by unit, in the order of the map's EEL 3111 path, checked against MIT 6.002
(docs/ocw/6-002.md). Unit 1 has its first lab.

## Textbooks

Kuphaldt, *Lessons in Electric Circuits*, Vol. I: DC (Design Science License), cited by topic through
`src/data/textbook.js`. Nothing is copied from it or from MIT 6.002: no prose, figures, examples or
exercises.

## What course material is here

Unit 1: node and mesh analysis of three resistive circuits of our own, each solved both ways. No UNF
syllabus or course pack was used.
