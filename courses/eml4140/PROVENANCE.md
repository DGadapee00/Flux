# Provenance

EML 4140 Heat Transfer lives in this folder. There is no earlier repo to snapshot. The course is
written here, unit by unit, in the order of the map's EML 4140 path, which follows MIT 2.51
(docs/ocw/2-51.md) and Lienhard and Lienhard's A Heat Transfer Textbook. Unit 1 has its first lab.

## Textbooks

Lienhard and Lienhard, *A Heat Transfer Textbook* (free to download), cited by topic through
`src/data/textbook.js`. Nothing is copied from it or from MIT 2.51: no prose, figures, examples or
exercises.

## What course material is here

Unit 1: the heat equation on a rod with fixed end temperatures, by a Fourier sine series and by
explicit finite differences. Diffusivities are standard property values at 300 K for pure copper,
pure aluminum, plain carbon steel and Pyrex glass. No UNF syllabus or course pack was used.
