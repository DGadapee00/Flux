# Provenance

EGN 3321 Dynamics lives in this folder. There is no earlier public repo to snapshot.
The course is written here, unit by unit. Units 1, 2, 3, 4, and 5 are in.

## What course material is here

Unit 1 is particle kinematics. The labs are rectilinear motion, normal and tangential
components, and relative motion on translating axes. The problems cover Hibbeler
Statics §1.3–1.6 (units, homogeneity, significant figures) and Hibbeler Dynamics
Chapter 12 (rectilinear motion, erratic graphs, projectiles, n–t, relative motion).
Each template cites the section in `src`. The numbers are generated. No textbook
example number is copied or invented.

Unit 2 is particle kinetics: force and acceleration. The labs are blocks and pulleys
(`fma`) and a banked curve with a vertical loop (`curve`). The problems cover
Hibbeler Dynamics Chapter 13. The numbers are generated and cited by section.

Unit 3 is work and energy. The labs are a collar on a rod (`collar`) and three paths
between the same ends (`paths`). The problems cover Hibbeler Dynamics Chapter 14.
The numbers are generated and cited by section.

Unit 4 is impulse and momentum. The labs are an F–t area (`impulse`), central and
oblique impact (`impact`), and a rocket at constant burn rate (`rocket`, §15.9).
The problems cover Hibbeler Dynamics Chapter 15: impulse, recoil, a bullet and a
block, central and oblique impact, angular momentum of a particle, and the rocket
in SI and in US customary units. The numbers are generated and cited by section.
Steady flow (§15.8) stays out.

Unit 5 is planar kinematics of a rigid body. The labs are an instantaneous centre
(`ic`: a ladder, a rolling wheel, and a four-bar), a slider-crank, and rolling
without slip. The problems cover Hibbeler Dynamics Chapter 16: belts and external
gears, absolute motion, relative velocity, the instantaneous centre, rolling, and
relative acceleration. The numbers are generated and cited by section. Rotating
axes (§16.8) stay out.

Unit 6 is planar kinetics of a rigid body. The labs are mass moments of composite
bodies (`inertia`), a bar or plate released about a pin (`pendulum`), and roll or
slip on an incline (`roll-slip`). The problems cover Hibbeler Dynamics Chapter 17:
mass moments, a crate that slides or tips, pin reactions, and roll or slip, in SI
and in US customary units. The numbers are generated and cited by section.

Unit 7 is work and energy of a rigid body. The labs are a hoop, disk, and sphere
racing down an incline (`race`) and a pinned bar or a rod in two slots
(`linkage`). The problems cover Hibbeler Dynamics Chapter 18: kinetic energy,
the work of a couple, and energy conservation for rolling bodies and linkages,
in SI and in US customary units. The numbers are generated and cited by section.

The Unit 1 notes are rewritten from the section list. They are not a transcription.
A `box fix` names a mistake a student would make by trusting a common slip, and it
cites the section.

## What is not here

The owner's course pack set the coverage and the order. It is copyrighted. It is not
in this folder: no PDF, no instructor notes, no worked example copied from the pack,
and no pack wording. There is no `incoming/` folder.

Sections the pack does not name stay out. See `CONVENTIONS.md`.

Student names, grades, and submissions are not in this folder.
