# Modern Physics: provenance

This is original introductory Flux material, written on 10 November 2026. Its short navigation
label is not an institutional course number. There is no instructor course pack or syllabus.
No textbook text, figures, tables or exercise statements are reproduced.

The unit notes link external references, record reading dates and describe the model limits:

- Unit 1: `Space, time and relativity`. References are linked in `src/notes/content/u1-notes.html`.
- Unit 2: `Photons and the photoelectric effect`. References are linked in `src/notes/content/u2-notes.html`.
- Unit 3: `Matter waves and diffraction`. References are linked in `src/notes/content/u3-notes.html`.
- Unit 4: `Atomic energy levels and spectra`. References are linked in `src/notes/content/u4-notes.html`.

The diagrams are generated from the lab models. The numeric and conceptual exercises are
written for Flux. Constants use rounded CODATA 2022 values from
https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Constants/. Numerical models live in
`packages/subjects/src/physics/foundations.js` and include independent checks.

These four units provide an introductory sequence, not a complete upper-level syllabus.
Advanced topics without labs are identified as further study in the notes. No raw course
materials are included or shipped.
