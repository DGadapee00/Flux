# Provenance

PHY 2048 is a review course. It has no Canvas drop and no instructor files in this folder.

## Source

Problems cite OpenStax *University Physics Volume 1* (CC BY 4.0):

https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1

Statements are written fresh, with generated numbers. A worked case uses numbers computed for this bank, and the citation is the section the method comes from. No figure and no textbook sentence is copied.

The chapter list follows that volume, keeping the chapters named in `docs/courses/phy2048.md`: vectors and kinematics (Ch 2–4), Newton's laws (Ch 5–6), work and energy (Ch 7–8), momentum (Ch 9), rotation (Ch 10–11), equilibrium (Ch 12), gravitation (Ch 13), oscillations (Ch 15). Fluids, waves, and sound are not part of this course. Confirm the list against the UNF syllabus before treating it as final (`DECISIONS.md`, D7).

## What is in this folder

The catalog, and a problem bank for every chapter this course keeps: 2–13 and 15. Five labs are logic only: projectile, incline, track, collide, and rotor. Their numbers are computed in this folder. The scene is drawn when the physics shell moves, after 23 Oct 2026. Drag is left out of the projectile. Equilibrium, gravitation, and oscillations have no lab yet. There is no interface.

The shared mechanics functions live in `packages/subjects/src/mechanics/`. They are not course material.
