# Provenance

STA 3032 lives in this folder. The tree is a snapshot of the public repo
DGadapee00/stats at the commit in `SOURCE`
(`5ee91038940732abfc6605514702a42f28726d5e`). The old repo is frozen.

## What course material is here

The notes are rewritten from the instructor's notes, chapters 1–10, one file per chapter
under `src/notes/`. A slip in the source is kept as a correction and listed in `ERRATA.md`.

Printed tables are data in `src/stats/tables-data.js`. A worked solution quotes the printed
value. Grading also accepts the exact value.

Review-sheet questions are worked cases, in the sheet's order, in
`src/problems/bank/review.js`. The named set in `src/problems/sets.js` lists them in that
same order.

Textbook material beyond the class notes is marked with its Walpole section. Those examples
are lettered (2.A, 2.B). No textbook example number is invented.

The snapshot contains no PDF, slide, or Word file, and no `incoming/` folder.
