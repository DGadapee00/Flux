# Provenance

STA 3032 lives in this folder. The tree is a snapshot of the public repo
DGadapee00/stats at the commit in `SOURCE`
(`5ee91038940732abfc6605514702a42f28726d5e`). The old repo is frozen.

## What course material is here

- **Textbook.** Problems cite Walpole, Myers, Myers & Ye by section (Walpole §5.2). The 20 worked
  cases that reproduce a printed answer are textbook ones. The printed tables of Appendix A are data
  in `src/stats/tables-data.js`; a worked solution quotes the printed value, and grading also
  accepts the exact value.
- **Generated problems.** Every problem statement is written fresh and drawn with random numbers.
- **The class's conventions** (TI-84 quartiles, np(1 − p) ≥ 10, ŷ = a + bx) are kept, as facts about
  how the course works.

## Removed on 13 Oct 2026

The instructor of STA 3032 has not been asked about their material appearing here (decision D6 in the
platform `PLAN.md`), so it was taken out of the site and this folder:

- the rewritten notes for Chapters 1–10 (`src/notes/`) and Notes mode;
- the class's midterm review sheet as problems and a named set (`src/problems/bank/review.js`);
- the 115 worked cases and 71 problem citations taken from their notes' examples;
- lab presets built on their notes' examples, and the errata about their notes.

The generated problems those cases belonged to stay. If they agree later, the material can come back
from the frozen DGadapee00/stats repo at the commit above.

The folder contains no PDF, slide, or Word file, and no `incoming/` folder.
