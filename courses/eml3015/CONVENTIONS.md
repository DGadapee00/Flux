# EML 3015 conventions

- Answers print three significant figures, four when the leading digit is 1
  (`src/problems/engine.js`).
- Units are SI. Gaps are entered and shown in millimetres and converted to metres inside the lab.
- y runs up from the still wall. The moving plate is at y = h and moves in +x.
- Shear stress is the stress the fluid puts on the plate, reported as a positive magnitude.
- Fluids are Newtonian with viscosity fixed by temperature. Non-Newtonian fluids are left out of
  unit 1.
