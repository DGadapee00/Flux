# Provenance

EMA 3010 Materials Science lives in this folder. There is no earlier repo to snapshot. The course is
written here, unit by unit, in the order of the map's EMA 3010 path, which follows MIT 3.091's
recitations for bonding, structure, defects and diffusion (docs/ocw/). Unit 1 is in.

## Textbooks

OpenStax Chemistry 2e (CC BY 4.0), cited by topic through `src/data/textbook.js`. Nothing is copied
from it or from MIT 3.091: no prose, figures, examples or exercises.

## What course material is here

Unit 1: the bond-energy curve as a Mie potential set by its depth and bond length, the force as its
slope and the stiffness as its curvature (closed forms checked by bisection, integration and a
finite difference). The ion-pair bond lengths are the standard gas-phase values for NaCl, KCl and
LiF; argon's well uses the standard Lennard-Jones parameters. No UNF syllabus or course pack was
used.
