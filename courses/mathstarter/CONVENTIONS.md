# MATH STARTER conventions

These stay in the course. They are not copied into `packages/core` or the shell. Replace this file
for a real course with its own rules:

- Significant figures (the engine's `sig` setting in `src/problems/engine.js`).
- Units: SI, US customary or both, through `@flux/subjects/convert.js`.
- Sign conventions.
- Sections of the textbook left out, and why.
- Colour meanings added to the platform's (docs/COURSE-STANDARD.md, section 2).
