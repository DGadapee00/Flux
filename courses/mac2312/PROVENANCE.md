# Provenance and compatibility

The former Calculus II course is merged into `courses/mac2311` under D28.
Its original authored labs, notes, problems and math moved into that course.
Their source is OpenStax Calculus Volume 2 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0), cited by section.
No instructor or student material is included.

This folder serves a compatibility redirect. Its original browser storage keys remain
available to the hub backup importer/exporter. The merged course reads those keys without
deleting them, namespaces the problem IDs and preserves review records and saved seeds.
