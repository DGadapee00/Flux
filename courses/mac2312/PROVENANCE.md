# Provenance

MAC 2312 is a review course. It has no Canvas drop and no instructor files.

## Source

Problems and labs follow the section list of OpenStax *Calculus Volume 2*,
[openstax.org/details/books/calculus-volume-2](https://openstax.org/details/books/calculus-volume-2).

The book is CC BY 4.0: [creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

## What ships

Statements are written fresh. Numbers are generated. A worked case may use a section's setup, cited as `OpenStax Calc 2 §2.2`, and never an invented example number. No figures and no text are copied.

The notes in `src/notes/content/` follow the book's section list. Their prose, derivations, and worked examples are written here, and every number in them is recomputed.

## What does not ship

No worksheets, slides, exams, or notes from a course. No student names, grades, or submissions.

Progress stays in the browser under `flux.mac2312.problems.v1` and `flux.mac2312.exam.v1`. The notes panel remembers its width and height under `flux.mac2312.notes.width.v1` and `flux.mac2312.notes.full.v1`.
