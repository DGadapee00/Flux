# Provenance

EGN 3203 Modern Computation Methods lives in this folder. There is no earlier repo to snapshot. The
course is written here, unit by unit, in the order of the map's EGN 3203 path, which follows MIT 2.086
(docs/ocw/2-086.md). Unit 1 is in.

## Textbooks

Margalit and Rabinoff, *Interactive Linear Algebra* (Georgia Tech, GFDL), and Hefferon, *Linear
Algebra* (CC BY-SA), cited by topic through `src/data/textbook.js`. Nothing is copied from either:
no prose, figures, examples or exercises.

## What course material is here

Unit 1: matrices as maps (area by the determinant, checked by the shoelace formula; products checked
by mapping columns one at a time) and Gaussian elimination (back substitution checked by Cramer's
rule on the same triangle). The tiny-pivot example is a standard illustration of floating-point
round-off, worked here in our own words. No UNF syllabus or course pack was used.
