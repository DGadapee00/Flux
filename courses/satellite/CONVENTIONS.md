# Satellite conventions

- Answers print three significant figures, four when the leading digit is 1
  (`src/problems/engine.js`).
- Lengths in km, speeds in km/s, times in s (shown in hours and minutes). μ = 398,600.4418 km³/s²,
  Earth's radius 6378.137 km.
- Time is measured from perigee; angles from perigee in the direction of motion.
