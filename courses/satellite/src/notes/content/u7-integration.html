1Sensor payload

Turn a physical signal into a reading with stated uncertainty.

Method

  1. Say what you will measure and over what range.
  2. Choose a sensor and write how its output follows the quantity: sensitivity and offset.
  3. Design the conditioning: divider, amplifier or filter, so the signal fills the converter’s range.
  4. Find the resolution: the smallest change one converter step stands for.
  5. Plan calibration: known inputs, the fit, and the uncertainty left.
  6. Count the data: bits per sample times samples per orbit.

What to produce

A sensor model, calibration estimate and data budget.

Handoff to other subsystems

Send data volume to Communications and operating limits to Thermal. Keep the sample schedule within the power budget.

Review checklist

  • Range, sensitivity and resolution are stated.
  • Loading by the next stage is checked.
  • Calibration and its uncertainty are planned.
  • Sample rate, power and data volume are stated.
  • The space environment the sensor must survive is considered.
Worked example · build review

Choose a sensor and its measurement range. Map the signal to a voltage or digital count, check loading and resolution, and record calibration assumptions, sample rate, power and data volume.

2Integration

Make the seven subsystem budgets agree on one mission.

Method

  1. Write one mission case: orbit, payload and how long it runs.
  2. Bring the seven budgets together: mass, power, temperature, pointing and data.
  3. Find where they disagree and settle each disagreement.
  4. Change one thing, such as transmitter duty cycle, and trace it through Power, Thermal and the data returned.
  5. Name the margin that limits the mission.

What to produce

One shared mission report with linked subsystem evidence and a tradeoff study.

Final handoff

Hand over a consistent set of budgets, interface definitions, assumptions, unresolved risks and verification evidence for the complete system.

Review checklist

  • One mission case is stated.
  • All seven budgets use the same numbers.
  • Each disagreement and how it was settled is recorded.
  • A trade study traces one change through the budgets.
  • The limiting margin is named.
Worked example · build review

Record one mission case. Reconcile mass, energy, temperature, pointing and data budgets. Change transmitter duty cycle, then trace the effect through Power, Thermal and the returned data. Document the margin that limits the mission.

Use the checklist above to review the result. State each assumption and the evidence supporting each interface value.
