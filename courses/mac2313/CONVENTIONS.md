# MAC 2313 conventions

These stay in this course. They are not copied into `packages/core` or the math shell.

- Notation follows Merino. Points are (x, y) and (x, y, z). Curves are γ(t).
- The directional derivative is D_u f with a unit vector.
- θ is in [0, 2π).
- The cross product is only in R³.
- Checks compare two methods: analytic partials against differences, Fubini in both orders, the
  Jacobian included.
