# Provenance

EGN 3311 Statics lives in this folder. The tree is a snapshot of the public repo
DGadapee00/statics-sim at the commit in `SOURCE`
(`6ce522ee0e58646b632e94087456076a51678eb4`). The old repo is frozen.

## What course material is here

The labs and the 34 problem templates follow Hibbeler, Engineering Mechanics: Statics,
chapters 2–10. Worked cases carry numbers from four public problem decks. A template that uses those numbers
cites the section in its `src` field, for example `Part 1 · 2.1–2.2`. Twenty-seven
templates have a `src`.

Seven templates have no section cite. Four are conceptual checks (`ch2.concepts`,
`ch4.couple-concept`, `ch6.determinacy`, `ch10.parallel-axis`). Chapter 8 (three
templates) is written from the chapter's standard cases, not from a problem deck.

There are no lecture notes, slides, worksheets, or Canvas exports. The snapshot
contains no PDF, slide, or Word file, and no `incoming/` folder.
