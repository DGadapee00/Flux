# MAP 2302 provenance

This course contains original Units 1–4 notes, problem templates and labs, and a four-unit catalog. It was copied from the course-neutral math starter,
not another real course. No source wording, problem statements, figures or PDFs ship.

1. MIT OCW 18.03SC Differential Equations, Fall 2011, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0:
   https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-03sc-differential-equations-fall-2011/ . Its session
   sequence determines coverage/order. The ignored reference cache is not shipped.
2. Dalton selected Lebl on 1 Nov 2026 (D38). Notes on Diffy Qs, author's page
   https://www.jirka.org/diffyqs/ offers CC BY-SA 4.0 or CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Trench,
   Elementary Differential Equations, Trinity https://digitalcommons.trinity.edu/mono/8/ ,
   CC BY-NC-SA 3.0 Unported. Licences checked 1 Nov 2026; metadata/section references only.
   Problems and explanations are written fresh; cited worked cases reproduce printed formulas or answers. Qualitative cases identify properties of those formulas.
3. On 4 Nov 2026 Dalton shared the UNF MAP 2302 course schedule, which follows Nagle, Saff
   and Snider, Fundamentals of Differential Equations, 9th ed. (Pearson, 2018). Only its section
   numbers are recorded, in `src/data/syllabus.js`, with our own short titles. No text,
   problems or figures from the book, and no class dates, are copied.
4. The UNF supplement (Unit 1 §§11–13, Unit 2 §§10–13, Unit 3 §12, and the labs
   exact-substitution, first-order-models, variation, cauchy-euler, higher-order and
   laplace-rules) was written fresh. Its worked examples were derived by hand and each is
   checked by RK4 or quadrature. Its problem cases reproduce those notes examples, not printed
   source answers. The Lebl section numbers it cites (§§1.4, 1.5, 1.8, 2.1, 2.3, 2.5, 6.1)
   have not been opened against the book yet: the session that wrote them had no network
   access to jirka.org. Confirm them before setting a `checked` date.

Application data sources and their assumptions are in the spine: HARIO server
external dimensions, Eibach ERS spring rate, NIST WWVB carrier, Parliament clock facts.
No product imagery is copied; application objects are drawn and animated in Flux.
