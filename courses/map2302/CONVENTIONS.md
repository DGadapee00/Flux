# MAP 2302 conventions

- SI inside computations; convert sourced customary device data with shared conversion constants.
- Time t in seconds unless an explicit nondimensional time is shown. y′ means d y/d t.
- Displacement is measured from static equilibrium; moving-base coordinates and signs are stated.
- Electrical current follows the chosen passive series-loop convention; charge derivative is current.
- A pendulum beat is half an oscillation. Distinguish effective length from overall hardware length.
- Display three significant figures; retain full precision internally and compare floats tolerantly.
- Shared notes type scale 11/13/15/18/24 px; no course font-size overrides. Derive each result,
  link worked examples to labs, distinguish definition/theorem/example/warning boxes, and label
  learner-error boxes Common mistake. Use PHY 2049's parts, legend and TOC.
- Domains, initial data, uniqueness hypotheses, causal step/impulse conventions and Fourier
  endpoint conventions are explicit. A transfer function presumes zero initial conditions.
- No power-series/Frobenius or PDE unit. The UNF supplement (exact equations, substitutions,
  models, variation of parameters, Cauchy–Euler, higher order, annihilators, transform rules) sits
  inside Units 1–3 as Part IV or a closing section, mapped section by section in `src/data/syllabus.js`.
- Lebl and the four-unit OCW spine were confirmed by Dalton on 1 Nov 2026. No fabricated source cases or device-performance promises.
