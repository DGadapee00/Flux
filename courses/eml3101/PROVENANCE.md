# Provenance

EML 3101 Thermodynamics II lives in this folder. There is no earlier repo to snapshot. The course is
written here, unit by unit, from the map's EML 3101 path, checked against MIT 16.050 Thermal Energy
(docs/ocw/16-050.md). Unit 1 has its first lab.

## Textbooks

Cleynen, *Engineering Thermodynamics*, an open textbook, cited by topic through `src/data/textbook.js`.
Nothing is copied from it or from MIT 16.050: no prose, figures, examples or exercises.

## Data

Molar masses and specific heats come from the NASA polynomials already in
`packages/subjects/src/thermo/` (GRI-Mech 3.0 and NASA Glenn; see that folder's PROVENANCE.md).

## What course material is here

Unit 1: mixtures of nitrogen with a second gas, of our own choosing. No UNF syllabus or course pack
was used.
