# EML 3101 conventions

- Answers print three significant figures, four when the leading digit is 1
  (`src/problems/engine.js`).
- Temperatures in kelvin, pressures in kPa on screen and Pa inside, masses in kg, amounts in mol.
- y is a mole fraction, x a mass fraction. M is a molar mass in kg/mol inside, g/mol on screen.
- Gases are ideal and their specific heats vary with temperature (NASA polynomials).
